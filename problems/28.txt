40. Shop Bill with GST
A shop adds 18% GST on the price of the items a customer buys. Read two integers, one per line: price (the price of one item) and quantity (the number of items bought).

The subtotal is price × quantity. The GST amount is 18% of the subtotal; the shop ignores the paise, so use integer division and drop the fractional part. The total is the subtotal plus the GST amount.

Input Format
Line 1 contains price.
Line 2 contains quantity.
