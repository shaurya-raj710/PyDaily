Read the starting balance. 
Then read transactions one per line: a positive number is a deposit (add it to the balance), a negative number is a withdrawal of that many rupees, and 0 closes the session.
 A withdrawal is allowed only if the balance is enough (the balance may become exactly 0).
  Otherwise the withdrawal is declined and the balance does not change. 
  As soon as the third declined withdrawal happens, the card is blocked and the ATM stops reading; every remaining line is ignored. 
  The input always ends with a 0 line.
